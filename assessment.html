Predictive Burnout Risk Profiling Engine

Academic & Clinical Disclaimer: This module utilizes the Seibt & Kreuzfeld (2021) multiple linear regression model to predict the likelihood of burnout risk based on established operational and psychological stressors (explaining ~29% of risk variance). It does not administer the MBI-GS and should be used as a systemic profiling estimate, not a clinical diagnosis.

Psychosocial & Operational Stressors

1. Operational Effort (ERI-Q Short)

Scale: 1 = Strongly Disagree to 5 = Strongly Agree

2. Systemic & Interpersonal Rewards (ERI-Q Short)

Scale: 1 = Strongly Disagree to 5 = Strongly Agree

3. Inability to Recover (Validated FABA Scale)

Scale: 1 = Strongly Disagree to 4 = Strongly Agree

4. Overcommitment (Validated ERI-Q Intrinsic Scale)

Scale: 1 = Strongly Disagree to 4 = Strongly Agree
